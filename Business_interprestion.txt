 Phase 8 — Business Interpretation Note Card
1. What did we observe?

    TV: strong positive association with Sales.
    Radio: positive association with Sales.
    Newspaper: weak association with Sales.
    In multiple regression:
        TV coefficient ≈ 0.0447, p < 0.001
        Radio coefficient ≈ 0.1892, p < 0.001
        Newspaper coefficient ≈ 0.0028, p = 0.696

2. What do the coefficients mean?

Sales^=2.9791+0.0447(TV)+0.1892(Radio)+0.0028(Newspaper)

    A coefficient describes the change in predicted Sales for a 1-unit increase in that predictor, holding the other predictors constant.
    Coefficient ≠ correlation.
    A larger coefficient does not automatically mean greater business importance because scale and spending ranges matter.

3. What does the model tell the business?

    TV and Radio provide useful predictive information about Sales.
    Newspaper adds little evidence of an independent linear relationship after accounting for TV and Radio.
    TV + Radio is currently preferred because it has:
        slightly better cross-validated RMSE (~1.663)
        fewer predictors
        simpler interpretation.

4. What does RMSE tell us?

    CV RMSE ≈ 1.663 sales units.
    Predictions will not exactly match actual Sales.
    Individual errors can be positive or negative.
    RMSE represents the typical magnitude of prediction error and is always non-negative.

5. Can we use the model for budget decisions?

Yes — cautiously.

We can plug different TV/Radio spending combinations into the equation to perform scenario analysis and estimate predicted Sales.

But:

    Scenario prediction ≠ optimal budget allocation.

6. Why can't we claim an optimal budget?

The data is observational, so regression does not establish that changing advertising spending causes the predicted sales change.

True budget optimization would require additional information/evidence about:

    causal effects
    total budget constraints
    channel costs
    diminishing returns/non-linearity
    profitability/margins
    business constraints

7. Final business recommendation

    The analysis supports prioritizing TV and Radio as useful predictors of Sales, with Newspaper providing limited additional predictive/statistical evidence. The TV + Radio model can support sales prediction and advertising-spend scenario analysis, but it should not be used alone to claim causal effects or determine the optimal advertising budget. Further causal and economic analysis would be required for budget optimization.
