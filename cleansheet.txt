Before you join, keep these 5 anchors in your head:

    Business: Understand whether advertising spend is associated with Sales and whether the model can predict Sales.
    EDA: TV and Radio showed stronger relationships with Sales; Newspaper was weaker.
    Statistics: Coefficients describe associations; t-tests/p-values help assess evidence that coefficients differ from zero.
    Prediction: RMSE/MAE/R² evaluate predictive performance on unseen data.
    Validation: Cross-validation gives a more robust estimate than relying on one train/test split.
